# HopReach Contributor Licence Agreement

> **Draft — not legal advice, and not yet in force.** Written to answer a
> specific problem (below) rather than copied blind from a template. Have a
> solicitor read it before asking anyone to sign it. Until it is adopted,
> HopReach accepts no external contributions.

## Why this exists

HopReach is licensed **AGPL-3.0 with the Commons Clause**, with A13xB0 as sole
licensor. That works precisely because one party holds the copyright to all of
it.

The moment someone else contributes and keeps their copyright, three things
A13xB0 can do today stop being possible without tracking down every past
contributor and getting each one's agreement:

1. **Changing the licence at all** — including softening it, or dropping the
   Commons Clause.
2. **Offering HopReach commercially**, which the Commons Clause forbids
   licensees but not the copyright holder.
3. **Reusing the code in a closed-source product.** This is not hypothetical:
   hamreach is a proprietary variant in the same domain. AGPL code from an
   outside contributor cannot lawfully be moved into it.

None of that is a reason to refuse contributions. It is a reason to be explicit
about the terms *before* the first one arrives, when the only person it affects
is the one deciding — rather than afterwards, when it means asking someone to
sign away rights to work they have already done for free.

**2. You keep your copyright.** This is a licence, not an assignment. Your

**4. You are entitled to grant this.** Your contribution is your original work,
or you have the right to submit it under these terms. If your employer has

rights.

**5. Third-party material is disclosed.** If your contribution includes work you
did not write, you identify it and its licence, clearly and in the pull request
— not in a comment somewhere in the diff.

kind, to the extent permitted by law.

**7. No obligation.** A13xB0 is under no obligation to accept, merge or use any
contribution.

## What this means for you, plainly

You keep ownership of what you write. You are giving A13xB0 permission to use
it under licences other than AGPL — including in commercial and closed-source
products such as hamreach — without owing you a fee.

If that is not acceptable to you, please do not contribute. That is a completely
reasonable position, and it is much better said before the work than after it.

## Signing

State in your pull request:

Individual contributors sign for themselves. If you are contributing on behalf
of an employer, say so, and name someone authorised to agree on their behalf.
